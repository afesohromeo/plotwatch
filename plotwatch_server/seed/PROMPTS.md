# Demo photo prompts: Afesoh Residence

AI image prompts for the demo project's site photos (doc/04g step 7). Every
photo shows **the same plot**, taken from **different camera positions**, at
the stages the demo has reached.

## The site, the same in every photo
- **The plot:** about 500 m² (roughly 20 m wide by 25 m deep), red laterite
  soil, in Odza, a residential area of Yaoundé, Cameroon. It will hold a
  **5-storey residential building** (ground floor plus four upper floors) in
  reinforced concrete and cement blocks.
- **Neighbours:** one **two-storey house on each side** (left and right when
  you face the plot from the street), both with plain rendered walls in light
  neutral colours, flat or low-pitched roofs and small balconies.
- **Trees:** a **tall palm tree** near the front-left corner, a **large mango
  tree** at the back right, and **banana plants** along the back.
- **Street:** a red-earth residential road along the front.
- **Sky:** overcast, soft tropical daylight, a little haze.
- **Look:** a realistic, slightly imperfect handheld smartphone photo, natural
  colours, nothing staged, no dramatic lighting.
- **Never:** readable text, signs, logos, watermarks, clear faces (workers seen
  from behind, from afar or turned away).

## How to use them
- Generate them **in one conversation**, in order, with the same tool (ChatGPT
  image generation or Google Gemini). From the second image on, keep the
  sentence *"Same plot, same neighbouring houses and same trees as the
  previous images."* that each prompt starts with.
- Ask for **landscape 4:3**, about 1600 px wide. Save as JPG or PNG with the
  file names below, in `demo images/`.
- Regenerate any image with readable text, logos, a watermark or a clear face.
- Note the tool you used; it goes in `CREDITS.md` and the README.
