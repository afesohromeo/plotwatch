# Demo data credits

The demo project "Afesoh Residence" (doc/04g step 7) uses these files. They
are demo material, not real site evidence.

## Site photos (`photos/`)
Generated by the developer with **Google Gemini** image generation, from the
prompts in [`PROMPTS.md`](PROMPTS.md). They show no real building, person or
place: the plot, the building and the neighbouring houses are made up.

| File | Stage |
|---|---|
| `site_preparation_1.jpg`, `site_preparation_2.jpg` | Site preparation |
| `foundation_1.jpg`, `foundation_2.jpg`, `foundation_3.jpg` | Foundation |
| `ground_floor_1.jpg`, `ground_floor_2.jpg`, `ground_floor_3.jpg` | Ground floor: columns and slab |

## Receipts (`receipts/`)
Drawn by the project with Flutter (`plotwatch_flutter/tool/screenshots/receipts_test.dart`).
The shops, phone numbers and invoice numbers are made up.

## People
The demo people (Brice Afesoh, Joseph Mvondo, Mary Aelys) are fictional.
